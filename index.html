Welcome to My Outdoor Guide

Ontario has many beautiful outdoor spaces. I enjoy learning about different places where people can go for a walk, relax, and enjoy fresh air.

Exploring Forest Trails

A simple walk outdoors can be a nice break from a busy day. Forest trails offer quiet surroundings and a chance to appreciate nature.

My Hiking Checklist

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • A bottle of water
  • A small snack
  • A charged phone
  • Clothes for the weather

Some Simple Tips Before You Go

Before visiting a trail, I think it is important to check the weather and choose a route that matches your comfort level. It is also a good idea to let someone know where you are going.

Remember to keep outdoor spaces clean by taking your garbage with you. Respecting nature helps other people enjoy these places too.

You can find more information about parks and activities on the Ontario Parks website.
